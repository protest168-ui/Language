Hi, I’m Saunab 👋

Language learner. YouTuber. Word nerd.

I run Saunab languages, a YouTube channel about learning languages. I write my own video scripts, from deep dives like Swahili noun classes to more relaxed talking videos about Indigenous languages.

I’m learning languages myself while making the videos: Swahili (through French) and Russian, with daily vocab practice. I also love historical linguistics and comparing how different languages work.
